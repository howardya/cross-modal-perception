The point

Every blind spot is somebody else's specialism — until it isn't.

Press Everyone at once. Most of the bars go away: what one reader walks past, another was hired to stop at. That is the half of this everybody expects, and it is real.

The other half is the interesting one. Seven professional readers, eight sentences of attention each — fifty-six chances — and they land on distinct sentences out of . They overlap heavily. And sentences are reached by none of them:

Read that list back. Most of it is the company describing itself — what management attributes, expects, and is pleased to report. Seven readers trained to find what matters agree, without conferring, to skip the story the filing is telling about itself.

One item on the list is not narrative at all. Sentence 13 is a 75 basis point increase in the margin on the term loan — a hard, recurring cost, sitting one line below the covenant amendment that two readers open with. All seven walk past it. A shared blind spot is not the same thing as a safe one.

A sentence counts as attended when it is in the top eight this reader gives the most attention to — the study's own cut, not one invented for this page.
