The instrument

Watch them go

The filing is typeset once and never moves. Only the reading moves. Each thread descends its own lane, touching sentences in the order that reader reaches for them — biggest claim on their attention first.

What you are looking at

Order of reaching, not a gaze trace

No eyes were tracked. A reader's thread visits the sentences in descending order of how much of that reader's attention the sentence commands, so the sequence is a ranking of pull, not a timeline. Nobody read this document in five seconds, and the spacing between touches is even because rank has no clock.

The threads still cross, and that is the whole point: the disagreement is about where to look, and it is real in the data whether or not a stopwatch was ever involved.
