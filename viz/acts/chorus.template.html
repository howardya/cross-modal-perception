The instrument

Watch them go

The filing is set as continuous prose and never re-flows into a list. Only the reading moves. As a reader reaches each sentence — biggest claim on their attention first — that sentence takes on its weight in the type: how large it grows is how much of that reader's attention it commands, and its colour is how they read it.

What you are looking at

Order of reaching, not a gaze trace

No eyes were tracked. A reader arrives at the sentences in descending order of how much of that reader's attention each one commands, so the sequence is a ranking of pull, not a timeline. Nobody read this document in five seconds, and the spacing between arrivals is even because rank has no clock. Type size carries that same ranking, so the finished paragraph is a picture of one reader's attention with the clock taken out. With all seven running, size is the mean across the readers who have got there — the filing's average loudness, not any one person's.

Send all seven and the same paragraph swells in seven different places at once, and that is the whole point: the disagreement is about where to look, and it is real in the data whether or not a stopwatch was ever involved.
