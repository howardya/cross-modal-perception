No eyes were tracked. A reader arrives at the sentences in descending order of how much of that reader's attention each one commands, so the order is a ranking of pull and nothing else — it is the salience column sorted, which is why nothing on this page spends a second channel on it. Type size, optical size and brightness carry that same ranking, so a finished paragraph is a picture of one reader's attention with no clock in it at all.

Cross-Modal Perception. Seven reading mandates across five documents, five independent runs each — 175 scorings, reproducible at Krippendorff's α 0.79–0.98. Two of the five documents were written for this study; three are real SEC filings nobody here wrote. Every reading is checked against an expectation derived from the literature, and the three documents that fail that check are written up rather than tuned away.

No practitioner has yet checked any of this against their own reading. A mismatch would be the most useful result the project could produce.
