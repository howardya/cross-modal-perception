A ranking of pull, not a gaze trace

No eyes were tracked. A reader arrives at the sentences in descending order of how much of that reader's attention each one commands, so the order is a ranking of pull and nothing else — it is the salience column sorted, which is why nothing on this page spends a second channel on it. Type size and optical size carry that same ranking, so a finished paragraph is a picture of one reader's attention with no clock in it at all.

The seven are never averaged. A mean across readers would throw away 49% of the variation in this filing — the disagreement between readers about a given sentence is as large as the difference between sentences — and the resulting profile matches no reader's own top eight. Where all seven have to appear at once they appear as seven objects, above.
